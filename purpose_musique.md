# Knowledge Base Purpose File

## Dataset: musique

## Description
This knowledge base is a diverse collection of Wikipedia-based encyclopedic articles. It covers a wide array of factual topics, including geographical locations and administrative districts, historical events and empires, biographies of notable figures (politicians, royalty, artists, athletes), organizations and companies, sports, entertainment, science, and technology. The articles contain rich relational information, detailing entities' properties, origins, affiliations, and connections to one another.

## Purpose
The primary purpose of this knowledge base is to serve as a retrieval source for multi-hop question answering (QA). It is specifically designed to support complex queries that require reasoning across multiple documents or connecting disparate pieces of information. For example, a system might need to identify a specific person from one article, determine their affiliation or relationship from another, and finally retrieve a property of that related entity from a third article to formulate the final answer.
